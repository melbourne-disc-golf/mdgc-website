MDGC and Merri-bek City Council are trialling a new course at [Lorne Street Reserve](https://www.merri-bek.vic.gov.au/exploring-merri-bek/parks-and-gardens/find-a-park/lorne-street-reserve/), alongside the Merri Creek in Fawkner. 
The course starts near the Lorne Street entrance. 

## Trial course — feedback wanted

This temporary course was set up on 20 July 2026 for a [six-month trial](https://conversations.merri-bek.vic.gov.au/disc-golf/). Merri-Bek City Council would love to hear what you think during that time — your feedback will help decide whether the course becomes a permanent fixture.

To show the course is being used and enjoyed, please:

- **check in** using MDGC's [course check-in form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1G_qT3H_g1jmtHVg32GYBFm6WL-qk-Uh2-Utl96afwWI/viewform)
- **record your rounds** [on UDisc](https://udisc.com/courses/lorne-street-reserve-nydT)
- **provide feedback** to the council on the [Lorne Street Reserve Disc Golf Trial](https://conversations.merri-bek.vic.gov.au/disc-golf/) page.

## Sharing the park

Lorne Street Reserve is a shared, off-lead dog area. Please respect all park users and give them right of way. Never throw if people or animals are within range.
