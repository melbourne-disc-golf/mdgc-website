Located near Melbourne CBD under the iconic Westgate Bridge, **Stony Creek** is very popular with folks who live or work in the inner-west.

**If you've just heard about disc golf and want to learn more, please drop us an** [**email** ](mailto:info@melbournediscgolf.com)**or DM via** [**Facebook** ](https://www.facebook.com/melbournediscgolf)**or** [**Instagram**](https://www.instagram.com/melbournediscgolf/)**. Our local members will be more than happy to show you the ropes and get you started (for free!)**

The course dates back to 2017, when it had with 3 permanent baskets and 1 homemade basket.
Since then, it has undergone a major upgrade to expand to the current 9-basket layout with concrete teepads, thanks to the support of City of Maribyrnong.

## Features

- 9 holes

## Getting there

### By car

- Parking is available near the course at 310 Hyde Street, Yarraville.
- More parking is available in the neighboring area, including: Hughes, Lormer, Beverley and Vockler Streets.

### By public transport

- **Train** (**Yarraville** Station) - about 1.5 kms away
