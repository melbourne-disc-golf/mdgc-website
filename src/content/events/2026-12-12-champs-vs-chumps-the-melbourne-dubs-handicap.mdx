Grab a partner for a day of handicapped doubles at Llewellyn Park. Every team gets a handicap based on both players' ratings, so whether you're a seasoned champ or a happy chump, you've got a real shot at winning. Teams are split into two divisions, with a raw-score prize and a handicap prize in each. Winners get custom stamped discs.

Round 1 is Best Shot, with a max of 10 tee shots from each player.

Round 2 is Best Score, with a maximum of 10 holes counted from each player.

(Best Score means you both play the hole as singles, then choose whose score to keep).

BBQ lunch provided.
