The Bald Hill Hot Start on Jan 1st is traditionally the first tournament of the New Year globally and will again be run in 2027 on a modified Bald Hill layout.

This is part of the MDGC Summer Cup - a series of low-cost, no-frills events across the summer months. This will be a 2 round, single day PDGA event.
