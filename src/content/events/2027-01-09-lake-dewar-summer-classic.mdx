A 2-day/3 round disc golf tournament set in the scenic surroundings of Lake Dewar Discovery Camp, Victoria.

Camping is available for Saturday night at $20pp. This will be sold as a separate item in the club shop.

Format: 3 rounds of 18 modified holes (2 on Saturday and 1 on Sunday morning)

Schedule:

Saturday 9th: Player Meeting: 8:30am. Round 1 Starts @ 9am. Round 2 Starts @ 2pm.

Sunday 10th: Round 3 Starts @9:30am. Presentations @ 1pm.

Capacity: 72 (Rego opens Oct 28th 8pm & closes Jan 6th).

Lake Dewar "Base" Course Map is shown in the attached photos - the modified layout will be added to UDisc a couple of weeks prior to the event (note - this is a private camp, so please do not enter the grounds for practice ahead of the event).
