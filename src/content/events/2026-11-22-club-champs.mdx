The 2026 season finale. Who wants to wear the crown?

Club Champs runs on **Sunday 22 November**: morning qualifying rounds across two parks, then finals at [Bald Hill](/courses/bald-hill). 

## Schedule and venues

| Time | Venue | Divisions |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 9am | [Bicentennial Park](/courses/bicentennial) | Div A, Div B |
| 9am | [Ruffey Lake Park](/courses/ruffey-lake) | Div C, Div D, 50+, Female |
| 1pm | [Bald Hill](/courses/bald-hill) | Finals |

Morning rounds use 20-basket layouts.

A "cut" will happen after the morning round, with the leaders in each division progressing to the finals.

## How to qualify

Eligibility to play in the champs is based on performance in MDGC [Social Days](/events/social) - 2026 Season 2.

- You **must be an MDGC member**, and have played **at least 3 Social Days** in Season 2.
- Your division is based on the division you played the most Social Days in.
- 50+ and Female players can choose to compete in normal social-day division, if they prefer.
