The 2026 season finale. Who wants to wear the crown?

Club Champs runs on **Sunday 22 November**: morning qualifying rounds across two parks, then finals at [Bald Hill](/courses/bald-hill). 

## Schedule and venues

| Time | Venue | Divisions |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 9am | [Bicentennial Park](/courses/bicentennial) | Div A, Div B |
| 9am | [Ruffey Lake Park](/courses/ruffey-lake) | Div C, Div D, Female |
| 1pm | [Bald Hill](/courses/bald-hill) | Finals |

Morning rounds for Div A and Div B are on an 11-basket, 22-hole layout at Bicentennial Park. Div C, Div D and Female play the normal layout at Ruffey Lake Park.

A "cut" will happen after the morning round, with the leaders in each division progressing to the finals.

## How to qualify

Eligibility to play in the champs is based on performance in [2026 Season 2](/events/social/seasons/2026-2/rounds).
