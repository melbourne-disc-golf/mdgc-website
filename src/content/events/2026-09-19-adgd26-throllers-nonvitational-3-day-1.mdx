[Aussie Disc Golf Day](https://www.australiandiscgolf.com/aussie-disc-golf-day/) is Australia's biggest disc golf participation event: a nationwide virtual tournament held simultaneously at clubs and courses across the country. Every year, on the same weekend in September, players of all skill levels tee off together in their own communities, and their scores roll up into a single national leaderboard. It's a low-cost, competitive yet community-focused event.

MDGC is hosting four events across the ADGD weekend — this is **Day 1 at Bald Hill**.

### The layout

We're bringing back the hazardous layout from last year's ADGD. Lots of fun, and even more OB, was had by all.

Day 1 will see the same layout as last year. [Day 2](/events/2026-09-20-adgd26-throllers-nonvitational-3-day-2) will be a little more friendly, with some larger islands and some shorter tee pads — but rest assured, it will still be very difficult to land in bounds.

Two rounds of 20 holes.

### Eligibility

For the first few days, registration for Day 1 is for players **rated 915 and above** (F divisions rated over 875), while Day 2 is for players **under 915** (F divisions rated under 875). After a few days, registration for both days opens to all.

All divisions are offered on both days. We encourage you to enter the lowest possible division you're eligible for.

### Format

Two rounds of 20 holes (same day).
