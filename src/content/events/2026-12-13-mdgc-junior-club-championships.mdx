The MDGC Junior Club Championships are returning for a second year, on Sunday 13th December at Ruffey Lake Park.

This will be an friendly and casual event, open to anyone born in 2008 or later. From complete beginners wanting to try disc golf for the first time, to the young guns battling it out for the title of Club Champion.  

We will be running all junior age divisions (including separate female divisions). We expect the event to comprise 1 or 2 rounds of the Ruffey Lake course, with coaching and a barbecue lunch between.  

More details to follow, including info about how to register.
